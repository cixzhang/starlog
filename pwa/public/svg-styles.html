Starlog Devlog: Illustration Style

2026-09-27 — Explored 6 SVG decoration styles for journal entries. Chosen: Doodle / Sticker (#6). Playful hand-drawn icons with chunky outlines and a sticker halo — like stickers peeled onto the page. Full guide lives in the starlog skill.

1. Minimalist Line Art

Clean even-weight outlines, no fill. Current default.

2. Hand-Drawn Sketchy

Loose wobbly lines, slight overshoot. Feels hand-made.

3. Flat Geometric

Bold solid shapes, no outlines. Poster-like.

4. Continuous Line

One unbroken path. Elegant and abstract.

5. Ink Wash / Sumi-e

Brush strokes, translucent washes. Meditative.

6. Doodle / Sticker ✓ chosen

Chunky outlines, white halo, playful tilt.
